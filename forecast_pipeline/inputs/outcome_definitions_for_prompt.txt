- behavior_mean [0 to 100]: Mean intended likelihood of eating less meat, using lower-carbon transport, installing solar, flying less, discussing climate change, and donating to an environmental NGO.. Scale/details: 0 = not likely at all; 100 = extremely likely; higher means stronger pro-climate behavioral intentions.
- belief_post [0 to 100]: Perceived accuracy of the statement: Human activities are causing climate change.. Scale/details: 0 = not at all accurate; 100 = extremely accurate.
- concern_mean [0 to 100]: Mean climate-change concern, perceived seriousness, and importance relative to other U.S. issues.. Scale/details: 0 = low concern/seriousness/importance; 100 = high concern/seriousness/importance.
- distrust_post [0 to 100]: How much the respondent distrusts climate scientists.. Scale/details: 0 = not at all; 100 = very strongly; higher means more distrust.
- donation_ams [0 to 10]: Dollars donated from a $10 bonus to the American Meteorological Society.. Scale/details: $0 to $10.
- funding_perceptions [0 to 100]: Reverse-coded perception of federal spending on climate-change research.. Scale/details: 0 = perceives far too much funding; 50 = about right; 100 = perceives far too little funding. Higher means stronger perceived need for more funding.
- inst_trust_mean [0 to 100]: Mean trust in the EPA, NASA, NOAA, universities and colleges, and the federal government.. Scale/details: 0 = not at all; 100 = very strongly.
- newsletter_signup [0 to 1]: Proportion subscribing to the Talking Climate newsletter.. Scale/details: 0 = no signup; 1 = signup. The ATE is a probability difference.
- policy_general [0 to 100]: Support for the U.S. government doing more to reduce global warming.. Scale/details: 0 = strongly oppose; 100 = strongly support.
- policy_role_mean [0 to 100]: Mean agreement that climate scientists should communicate with policymakers, work with them, advocate for policies, and be involved in policymaking.. Scale/details: 0 = strongly disagree; 100 = strongly agree.
- policy_specific_mean [0 to 100]: Mean support for seven specific mitigation policies covering fossil-fuel taxes, public transport, sustainable energy, land protection, carbon-intensive food taxes, green jobs, and clean waterways.. Scale/details: 0 = strongly oppose; 100 = strongly support.
- trust_multidimensional [0 to 100]: Primary outcome: mean of climate-scientist competence, integrity, benevolence, and openness subscales.. Scale/details: 0 = very low multidimensional trust; 100 = very high multidimensional trust.
- trust_post [0 to 100]: How much the respondent trusts climate scientists.. Scale/details: 0 = not at all; 100 = very strongly.
